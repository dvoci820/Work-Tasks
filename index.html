Survey Wrap Up: What Went Well

I figured out a way to use AI to help generate manager and department level survey spreadsheets. It does not do 100% of the work, but it saved an estimated 10 to 20 minutes per manager by creating much of the survey breakdown automatically.

Survey Wrap Up: What Did Not Work

SurveyMonkey could not fully support the dashboard approach. The goal was to build all manager dashboards in SurveyMonkey, but once we reached 65 dashboards, the system became too slow to work with efficiently. The AI generated spreadsheet approach ended up being faster and may be the better path next year.

Survey Reporting Output

Survey output included 55 manager spreadsheets, 7 division level spreadsheets, 36 department spreadsheets, and 65 SurveyMonkey dashboards. Survey reporting time was reduced by roughly 33% year over year through AI assisted spreadsheet generation.
